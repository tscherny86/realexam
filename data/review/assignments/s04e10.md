# s04e10 – S04 - 10 Future

Show notes: Native English speakers answer questions about the future.

This topic is one that often comes up in exams. You can be asked about your personal plans or goals, or about predictions for the future, regarding things like jobs, Artificial Intelligence or maybe the environment. 

In this episode you’ll hear some great ways to express what you would like to do, you’ll hear what skills will be useful for the future, you will learn some nice adverbs, phrasal verbs and some top-class vocabulary too, including some super-useful idioms.
